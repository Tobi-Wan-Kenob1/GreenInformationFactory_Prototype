# Target journals — analysis, 2026-11-21

**Constraints.**
- Interdisciplinary.
- Published by Elsevier, Springer Nature or Wiley, so covered by the DEAL
  agreements.

**What DEAL means here.**
- The University of Bayreuth joined all three DEAL agreements for 2024–2028.
- In **hybrid** (subscription) journals, eligible corresponding authors publish
  open access **with no article charge**:
  - about 1 800 Elsevier core hybrid titles;
  - more than 2 000 Springer Nature titles, excluding Nature-branded journals;
  - about 1 300 Wiley titles.
- **Fully open-access (gold)** journals get only a discount, about 20 % off list
  price. The rest falls to the university's Open Access Publication Fund.
- Hybrid journals are therefore the cost-free route.
- Terms to confirm with the library (oa@uni-bayreuth.de):
  - the corresponding author must be at a participating institution;
  - Elsevier has charged a €100 net service fee per article since 2024;
  - Bayreuth's DEAL pages are marked as being revised.

**What the paper is.**
- A pre-registered back-test of a policy–funding topic tool: research policy,
  foresight methods, text mining of EU records, and a bioeconomy and raw
  materials domain.
- The result is mixed and modest: the classification carries information, the
  ranking does not.
- Its strongest selling points are the method (frozen corpora, rule-based
  queries, one-shot analysis, open everything) and the honest account of the
  corrections.
- Journals that value methods and transparency over a positive headline suit it
  best.

## Recommendation

| Rank | Journal | Publisher · OA model | Fit | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Technological Forecasting and Social Change** | Elsevier · hybrid (DEAL: no charge) | Its stated scope is the methodology and practice of technological forecasting and futures studies, and their societal effects. A back-test of a forecasting-type tool is squarely that. Interdisciplinary readership; high profile (JIF 12.0 for 2022). | Large and competitive. Reviewers will want the foresight literature engaged, and a clear methods contribution beyond one tool. |
| **2** | **Futures & Foresight Science** | Wiley · hybrid (DEAL: no charge) | Devoted to advancing methods for anticipating the future. A protocol for back-testing a foresight tool is a methods paper in exactly its sense. Smaller, and the closest fit. | Lower visibility than TFSC. Less read in research policy and environmental policy. |
| **3** | **Environmental Science & Policy** | Elsevier · hybrid (DEAL: no charge) | Research on the science–policy–society interface and on knowledge production for decision-making. Puts the paper in front of the bioeconomy and environmental-governance audience the tool is built for. | Expects more than stated policy relevance: the discussion must speak to the environmental-policy use of such tools, not only to their validity. |
| 4 | **Scientometrics** | Springer · hybrid (DEAL: no charge) | Quantitative study of science and research funding. Text mining of CORDIS fits its methods core (JIF 3.5 for 2023). A safe home for the method. | More disciplinary than interdisciplinary. The policy argument would be under-read. |
| 5 | **Research Policy** | Elsevier · hybrid (DEAL: no charge) | The leading multi-disciplinary research and innovation policy journal. | A reach. It expects implications for policy or management theory, and a tool-validation study with a modest result is unlikely to clear that bar without reframing. |
| — | Humanities and Social Sciences Communications | Springer Nature · **gold** (DEAL: discount only; list APC about €1 590) | Broad, interdisciplinary, judged on sound method. | Costs money despite DEAL, unless the OA fund pays. A fallback, not a first choice. |

**Suggested order:**
1. Submit to **Technological Forecasting and Social Change**.
2. If rejected without review, go to **Futures & Foresight Science**.
3. Choose **Environmental Science & Policy** instead of either if you want the
   bioeconomy-policy audience over the foresight one. That choice also changes
   how the discussion should be weighted.

## What each choice changes in the manuscript

- **TFSC.**
  - Frame it as forecasting-tool evaluation: back-testing, baselines,
    out-of-sample.
  - Engage the technology-foresight and horizon-scanning literature.
  - Keep the Tier B cases short.
- **FFS.**
  - Lead with the protocol as a reusable method.
  - The registered results become the worked demonstration.
- **ESP.**
  - Lead with the science–policy interface and what the result means for
    environmental and bioeconomy policy work.
  - Give the Tier B cases more room.

## Not verified here

Check these on each journal's own author pages before submitting:
- word limits;
- current impact factors (the figures above are the latest found, from 2022
  and 2023);
- whether a pre-registered study can be flagged as such at submission.

None of these journals was found to offer a formal Registered Report track.
